Delete your TopOne account

This page explains how to delete your account and data from the TopOne app (Top One Class Limited).

Delete it yourself, in the app

The quickest way is in the app itself:

  1. Open the TopOne app and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm when asked

Deletion happens straight away. It cannot be undone, and there is no recovery period — once it is done, the data is gone.

If you cannot access the app

If you have uninstalled the app, forgotten your password, or cannot sign in for any reason, email enquiriesrichmond@topone.co.uk from the address you used to register, with the subject "Delete my account".

We will delete your account and confirm by email within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Everything the app holds about you:

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your first name, last name, and phone number
  • Your enquiries and all messages in them
  • Access to the account on every device you are signed in on

What is kept, and why

If you have already booked an appointment, a member of salon staff will have entered that appointment into Phorest, the salon's main booking system. That booking record is separate from the app and is not removed when you delete your app account, because it is the salon's own record of an appointment you made.

To have that record removed as well, email enquiriesrichmond@topone.co.uk and ask us to delete your salon booking records too.

The app does not take payments, so there are no card or payment records to keep.

Enquiries are deleted automatically too

Even if you keep your account, enquiries do not stay forever:

  • Enquiries closed by the salon are deleted 14 days after they close
  • Enquiries left open are deleted 90 days after the last message
