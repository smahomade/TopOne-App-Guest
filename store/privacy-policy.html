This policy explains what the TopOne app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

When you create an account and use the app:

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressSigning in, password resets, account confirmation
PasswordSigning in. Stored only as a secure hash — we never see it
First and last nameIdentifying your booking to salon staff
Phone numberContacting you about your appointment

We do not collect your device location, contacts, photos, or camera. We do not use advertising or tracking technology, and we do not build advertising profiles.

You can browse the app as a guest without an account. In that case we collect none of the above.

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

  • Contract — account details and enquiries, so we can provide the booking service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure and working.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers that run the service for us:

  • Supabase — database, account storage, and authentication. Your data is held in the United Kingdom (London) region.
  • Resend — sending account confirmation and password-reset emails. This involves your email address being processed outside the UK (United States) under the safeguards in their data processing terms.

These providers act on our instructions.

Salon staff read and reply to your enquiries in the app, so they can see your name, contact details, and messages. When your booking is agreed, a member of staff enters your appointment details into Phorest, the salon's main booking system, where the appointment is then managed. The app itself does not send anything to Phorest automatically.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, contact details, and enquiry history from the app immediately and permanently.

Once an enquiry has been closed by the salon, it is kept for 14 days and then automatically deleted, along with the messages in it. The app shows you a countdown of how long a closed enquiry has left.

Enquiries that are still open are automatically deleted 90 days after the last message in them. An enquiry you are still using is not affected — the 90 days only begins once the conversation goes quiet.

Once a member of staff has entered your appointment into Phorest, that booking record is held in Phorest under the salon's own retention rules. Deleting your app account does not remove it, because it is a separate record of an appointment you booked.

The app does not take payments and holds no card or financial details.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, in the app: Profile → Delete Account. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email enquiriesrichmond@topone.co.uk and we will delete it for you. Full details are on our account deletion page.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or transfer it elsewhere. Email enquiriesrichmond@topone.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
